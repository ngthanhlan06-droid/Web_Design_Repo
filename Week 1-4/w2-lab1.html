About CORTIS

CORTIS is a South Korean boy group under BIGHIT MUSIC. The group consists of five members: Martin, James, Juhoon, Seonghyeon, and Keonho.

CORTIS is known for its youthful energy, creative style, and unique approach to music. The members participate in different aspects of their artistic work and aim to create music that represents their own generation.
