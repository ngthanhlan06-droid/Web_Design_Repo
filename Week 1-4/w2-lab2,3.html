Overview

National Economics University (NEU) is one of the leading universities in Vietnam. It specializes in economics, business, finance, management, and other related fields.

Core Features

NEU offers a wide range of programs and courses, including undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs. The university is known for its strong academic reputation, experienced faculty, and modern facilities. It also has partnerships with international universities and organizations, providing students with opportunities for exchange programs and internships.
